8 Days Private tour to Mara River Crossing Experience

Discover the very best of Tanzania on an unforgettable 8-day safari through Tarangire, Central
Serengeti, Northern Serengeti and the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater.

Explore Tarangire's beautiful landscapes, famous for its giant baobab trees and impressive
elephant herds, before heading into Central Serengeti, where endless savannah plains offer
excellent opportunities to encounter Africa's iconic wildlife.

Continue to Northern Serengeti, the highlight of your adventure, where you will follow the
spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration and experience the excitement of the Mara River, with
opportunities to witness thousands of wildebeest crossing its crocodile-inhabited waters.

Throughout your safari, enjoy exceptional opportunities to encounter Africa's great predators,
including lions, leopards and cheetahs. Complete your Big Five experience with a visit to
Ngorongoro Crater, where you will descend into this remarkable natural wildlife sanctuary in
search of elephants, buffaloes, lions, leopards and the endangered black rhino, with a possibility
of spotting the rare white rhino.

This extraordinary safari combines breathtaking landscapes, remarkable wildlife encounters, the
excitement of the Great Migration and the unforgettable opportunity to experience Tanzania's
incredible natural heritage.

Day By Day

Day 1: Arrival in Arusha - Welcome to Tanzania

Upon arrival at either Arusha Airport or Kilimanjaro International Airport, depending on your
flight arrangements, you will be warmly welcomed by our driver and transferred to your
accommodation in Arusha in a comfortable private vehicle. Later, meet your safari consultant for
a detailed safari briefing, review your itinerary and finalize any outstanding payments. Enjoy the
rest of the day relaxing as you prepare for your exciting Tanzania wildlife adventure.

Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge
Day 2: Arusha to Tarangire National Park

After breakfast, depart Arusha for approximately 2.5 hours to Tarangire National Park. Upon
arrival, begin your wildlife adventure with an exciting game drive through this beautiful
wilderness, famous for its impressive elephant herds, ancient baobab trees and diverse wildlife.

Explore the park in search of large elephant families, lions, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest and
different species of antelopes. The Tarangire River also provides an important water source,
attracting numerous animals and creating excellent wildlife-viewing opportunities.

In the late afternoon, leave the park and drive approximately 45 minutes to your accommodation.
Enjoy dinner and a relaxing evening.

Accommodation: Marera Valley Safari Lodge

Day 3: Karatu to Central Serengeti

After breakfast at Marera Valley Lodge, depart Karatu and journey towards the world-famous
Serengeti National Park through the spectacular Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Enjoy a scenic transfer game drive of approximately four hours towards Central Serengeti, with
opportunities to stop and observe wildlife along the way. As you enter the endless plains, prepare
to discover one of Africa's richest wildlife habitats.

Central Serengeti is particularly famous for its large populations of predators. Spend your
afternoon exploring the plains in search of lions, leopards and cheetahs, alongside elephants,
buffaloes, giraffes and other fascinating wildlife.

Accommodation: Friend Serengeti Camp
Day 4: Central Serengeti to Northern Serengeti

After an early breakfast, depart Central Serengeti and travel towards Northern Serengeti on an
exciting transfer game drive of approximately three hours, depending on wildlife sightings and
road conditions.

Along the way, enjoy opportunities to observe wildlife and appreciate the changing landscapes of
the Serengeti. Your journey takes you towards the northern wilderness, where the Great
Wildebeest Migration gathers around the Mara River ecosystem.

Upon arrival, explore Northern Serengeti in search of enormous herds of wildebeest and zebras,
together with the predators that follow their movements. This region also offers excellent
opportunities to encounter lions, leopards, cheetahs and other wildlife.

Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp

Day 5: Northern Serengeti -Mara River Crossing Experience

After an early breakfast, head towards the famous Mara River, where you will spend the day
exploring the migration routes and searching for the spectacular wildebeest river crossings.

Witness thousands of wildebeest and zebras gathering along the riverbanks as they navigate their
journey in search of fresh grazing grounds. If fortunate, you may experience one of nature's most
dramatic spectacles as massive herds plunge into the crocodile-inhabited waters, creating
unforgettable moments in the African wilderness.

The river crossing experience is particularly sought after between late July and September,
although actual timing depends on rainfall and migration movements.

Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp
Day 6: Northern Serengeti - A Full Day Following the Great Migration

Enjoy an early breakfast before heading out for another full day of wildlife exploration in
Northern Serengeti.

Continue following the Great Wildebeest Migration around the Mara River, observing the
movements of the herds and searching for opportunities to witness further river crossings.

Beyond the migration, explore the remarkable wildlife of Northern Serengeti, particularly the big
cats that follow the migrating herds. Look out for lions, leopards and cheetahs, as well as
crocodiles, elephants, giraffes and other fascinating wildlife.

This day offers another excellent opportunity to experience the beauty, excitement and
unpredictability of the African wilderness.

Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp

Day 7: Northern Serengeti to Ngorongoro Highlands

After breakfast, enjoy a morning game drive in Northern Serengeti before beginning your
journey towards the Ngorongoro Highlands.

Travel south through the Serengeti with a transfer game drive, enjoying opportunities to explore
different wildlife habitats along the way. Depending on wildlife sightings and your route, you
may explore parts of Central Serengeti, offering further opportunities to encounter lions,
leopards, cheetahs and other wildlife.

Continue towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, enjoying the spectacular scenery as the
endless Serengeti plains gradually give way to the beautiful highlands.

Arrive at your accommodation in the Ngorongoro Highlands for dinner and overnight, preparing
for your final Big Five adventure.

Accommodation: Embalakai Ngorongoro Camp
Day 8: Ngorongoro Crater

After an early breakfast, descend approximately 600 metres into the magnificent Ngorongoro
Crater for an unforgettable game drive across one of Africa's most remarkable wildlife
destinations.

Explore the crater's diverse habitats in search of the remaining members of the Big Five,
particularly the endangered black rhino. The crater is also home to large populations of lions,
elephants, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeest and numerous other wildlife species.

With approximately 6-7 hours of game viewing, enjoy an exceptional opportunity to complete
your Big Five experience while appreciating the spectacular landscapes of this natural wildlife
sanctuary.

In the afternoon, ascend the crater and drive back to Arusha, where you will either be transferred
to your accommodation or directly to Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport,
depending on your flight arrangements.

Important flight recommendation: If departing on this day, please book a late-evening flight to
allow sufficient time for your Ngorongoro Crater game drive, the ascent and the transfer to the
airport.
