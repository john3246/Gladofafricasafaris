8 Days Fly Out from Northern Serengeti Mara River Crossing To Zanzibar

Discover the very best of Tanzania's wildlife on an unforgettable 8-day safari through Tarangire
National Park, Ngorongoro Crater, Central Serengeti and Northern Serengeti.

Begin your adventure in Tarangire National Park, famous for its magnificent elephant herds,
ancient baobab trees and diverse wildlife. Continue to Central Serengeti, where the endless
savannah plains offer exceptional opportunities to encounter Africa's iconic big cats, including
lions, leopards and cheetahs.

The highlight of your journey awaits in Northern Serengeti, where you will follow the
spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration and explore the legendary Mara River in search of
dramatic river crossings. Conclude your wildlife adventure with an exciting game drive in
Ngorongoro Crater, offering opportunities to encounter the Big Five, including the endangered
black rhino.

To make your onward journey more convenient, this safari includes a flight from Northern
Serengeti directly to Zanzibar, eliminating the need for a long road transfer back to Arusha or
Kilimanjaro. This allows you to save valuable travel time and continue your holiday in Zanzibar
with greater comfort and convenience.

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN ARUSHA - WELCOME TO TANZANIA

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport, you will be warmly
welcomed by our representative and transferred to your accommodation in Arusha.

Depending on your arrival time, meet your safari consultant for a personal safari briefing, review
your itinerary and receive important information about your upcoming adventure. The remainder
of the day is yours to relax and prepare for the exciting wildlife journey ahead.

Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge
DAY 2: ARUSHA TO TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK - THE LAND OF GIANTS

After breakfast, depart Arusha for a scenic journey of approximately 2.5 hours to Tarangire
National Park.

Upon arrival, enjoy an exciting game drive through a landscape characterized by ancient baobab
trees, open savannah and the magnificent Tarangire River. The park is particularly famous for its
impressive elephant population, offering wonderful opportunities to observe large family herds
in their natural environment.

Explore the wilderness in search of lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, wildebeest and different
species of antelopes. Tarangire also offers excellent opportunities for birdwatching and wildlife
photography.

After an exciting day in the park, proceed to your accommodation in Karatu for dinner and
relaxation.

Accommodation: Marera Valley Safari Lodge

DAY 3: Ngorongoro crater

Begin your morning with an early breakfast before departing for the magnificent Ngorongoro
Conservation Area.

Descend approximately 600 metres into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable
game drive across its remarkable wildlife-rich floor. Explore its open grasslands, freshwater
lakes and woodland habitats in search of Africa's Big Five.

The crater offers excellent opportunities to encounter lions, elephants, buffaloes and leopards,
alongside the endangered black rhino, one of the most sought-after wildlife sightings in
Tanzania.

After approximately 5-6 hours of game viewing, ascend the crater and continue towards
Serengeti National Park through the beautiful Ngorongoro Highlands.

Accommodation: Friend Serengeti Camp
day 4: Central Serengeti
Today is dedicated to exploring the extraordinary wildlife of Central Serengeti, a region
renowned for its remarkable predator populations and exceptional game-viewing opportunities.
After breakfast, venture into the plains for a full-day game drive, exploring different wildlife
territories in search of Africa's iconic big cats.
Look out for magnificent lions resting on rocky kopjes, elusive leopards hidden among acacia
trees and cheetahs scanning the open grasslands for their next hunting opportunity.
Beyond the predators, enjoy opportunities to encounter elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras and
numerous other wildlife species.
Take your time exploring the beautiful Serengeti landscapes, with every game drive offering the
possibility of remarkable wildlife encounters and memorable photographic moments.
Return to your accommodation for dinner and overnight.
Accommodation: Friend Serengeti Camp
day 5: Central Serengeti to Northern Serengeti
After breakfast, depart Central Serengeti and travel north towards the spectacular Northern
Serengeti on an exciting transfer game drive.
Enjoy the changing landscapes as you journey through the endless plains, stopping along the way
to observe wildlife and appreciate the natural beauty of the Serengeti ecosystem.
As you approach Northern Serengeti, the excitement builds as you enter the region of the famous
Mara River, an important gathering area for the migrating wildebeest and zebras.
Spend the afternoon exploring the northern plains in search of enormous migration herds and the
predators that follow their movements, including lions, leopards and cheetahs.
Arrive at your accommodation in Northern Serengeti for dinner and overnight, preparing for the
remarkable migration experience ahead.
Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp
Day 6: Northern Serengeti - Mara river crossing experience

Enjoy an early breakfast before heading towards the legendary Mara River for a full day of
wildlife exploration.

Your main focus today is following the Great Wildebeest Migration and searching for
opportunities to witness the dramatic river crossings.

Observe thousands of wildebeest and zebras gathering along the riverbanks as they search for
opportunities to cross into fresh grazing territories. If fortunate, you may witness the
breathtaking spectacle of massive herds plunging into crocodile-inhabited waters, navigating
powerful currents and steep riverbanks.

Your experienced safari guide will follow the movements of the herds and explore promising
river-crossing locations throughout the day.

This is one of the most extraordinary wildlife experiences in Africa, offering exceptional
photographic opportunities and unforgettable moments in the wilderness.

Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp

Day 7: northern Serengeti - another full day following the great migration

Wake up to another exciting day in the northern wilderness. After breakfast, head out for a full-
day game drive around the Mara River and surrounding migration areas.

Having another dedicated day in Northern Serengeti provides additional opportunities to follow
the migrating herds, observe their movements and search for spectacular river crossings.

Beyond the migration, explore the surrounding plains in search of lions, leopards, cheetahs and
other predators following the migrating herds. You may also encounter elephants, giraffes,
crocodiles and numerous other wildlife species.

Enjoy the magnificent scenery and extraordinary atmosphere of Northern Serengeti, where every
game drive brings a new opportunity to experience the wonders of the African wilderness.

Return to your accommodation for your final dinner and overnight in Serengeti.

Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp
Day 8: Northern Serengeti - Morning game drive and flight to zanzibar
Enjoy your final morning in the wilderness with an early breakfast followed by a memorable
game drive in Northern Serengeti.
Take advantage of your last opportunity to explore the northern plains, searching for the Great
Migration herds and the magnificent wildlife that makes this region so special.
After your morning game drive, proceed to the designated airstrip, where you will board your
scheduled afternoon flight to Zanzibar.
Flight: Northern Serengeti to Zanzibar.
