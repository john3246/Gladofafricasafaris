7 Day Mid-range Tour to Northern Serengeti Wildebeest Migration

Experience the magic of Tanzania's wilderness on a remarkable 7-day mid-range safari designed
around the spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration in Northern Serengeti. This unforgettable
adventure combines exceptional wildlife encounters, breathtaking landscapes and comfortable
mid-range accommodation, bringing you closer to one of the greatest natural spectacles in
Tanzania Savanna.

Explore the wildlife-rich plains of Tarangire National Park, discover the magnificent
Ngorongoro Crater and journey into the legendary Serengeti ecosystem, where endless savannah
plains provide a home to Africa's most iconic wildlife.

The highlight of your adventure is Northern Serengeti, where you will follow the movements of
thousands of wildebeest and zebras as they journey across the plains in search of fresh grazing
grounds. Spend valuable time exploring the famous Mara River ecosystem, with opportunities to
witness the dramatic river crossings that make this migration one of Africa's most extraordinary
wildlife experiences.

Throughout your safari, enjoy opportunities to encounter lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants,
giraffes and numerous other wildlife species. At Ngorongoro Crater, explore a remarkable
natural sanctuary with opportunities to encounter the Big Five, including the endangered black
rhino.

After exciting days of game drives, relax in carefully selected mid-range lodges and tented
camps that combine comfort, welcoming hospitality and an authentic African safari atmosphere.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport - Transfer to Arusha

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your safari
representative and transferred to your hotel in Arusha. During the drive, enjoy views of the
beautiful Tanzanian countryside, with its green landscapes, local villages and, weather
permitting, distant views of Mount Meru.
Arusha is the main gateway to Tanzania's famous Northern Safari Circuit and an ideal starting
point for your wildlife adventure. After checking in, you will have time to relax and recover from
your international journey. Depending on your arrival time, you may also receive a short safari
briefing to prepare for the adventure ahead.

Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge

Day 2: Arusha - Tarangire National Park - Karatu

After breakfast, depart Arusha and drive towards Tarangire National Park for your first wildlife
experience. The journey takes you through the scenic landscapes of northern Tanzania before
entering a park famous for its impressive elephant population, ancient baobab trees and diverse
wildlife.

Tarangire offers a distinctive safari atmosphere, with open savannah, seasonal swamps, acacia
woodlands and the Tarangire River, which provides an important water source for wildlife,
particularly during the dry season.

During your game drive, look out for large elephant families moving through the park,
sometimes gathering beneath the enormous baobab trees. Other wildlife commonly found here
includes giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, buffaloes, impalas and warthogs. With some luck, you may
also encounter predators such as lions, leopards and cheetahs.

Tarangire is also an excellent destination for bird enthusiasts, with a wide variety of colourful
and fascinating bird species. Enjoy a picnic lunch at a designated site before continuing your
afternoon game drive.

In the late afternoon, exit the park and continue towards Karatu, a charming highland town
surrounded by fertile farmland and beautiful green landscapes near the Ngorongoro Conservation
Area.

Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge
Day 3: Karatu - Central Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, depart Karatu and travel towards the world-famous Serengeti National Park
through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. This journey offers beautiful scenery as you move
from the cooler highlands towards the vast open plains of the Serengeti.

The Serengeti is one of Africa's most celebrated wildlife destinations, famous for its endless
grasslands, remarkable biodiversity and the Great Wildebeest Migration. Its diverse habitats
support an impressive range of wildlife throughout the year.

Upon entering the Central Serengeti, particularly the Seronera region, begin exploring one of the
park's most rewarding wildlife areas. This region is known for its resident wildlife and strong
predator population, making it an excellent place to search for lions, leopards and cheetahs.

The Seronera River and its surrounding woodland provide important habitats for wildlife, while
the nearby plains attract large numbers of grazing animals. You may also visit the famous
Seronera Hippo Pool, where hippos can often be observed resting together in the water.

Enjoy your game drive as you explore the Central Serengeti's diverse landscapes, looking for
elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeest and many other species.

Accommodation: Friend Serengeti Camp

Day 4: Central Serengeti - Northern Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, begin your journey towards the Northern Serengeti, enjoying a game drive along
the way. This is an exciting day as you travel through different sections of the Serengeti
ecosystem, following the seasonal movements of wildlife.

The Northern Serengeti is particularly famous for the Great Wildebeest Migration, especially the
areas surrounding the Mara River. During the migration season, enormous herds of wildebeest
and zebras move through this region in search of fresh grazing and water.

The landscape becomes increasingly varied as you travel north, with open grasslands, rolling
hills, rocky outcrops and riverine vegetation. These habitats support resident wildlife as well as
migratory herds, offering opportunities to encounter elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, lions, leopards
and other animals.
As you approach your accommodation, your guide will continue searching for wildlife and
assessing activity in the surrounding migration areas. The exact location of the herds changes
throughout the season, so your guide will use local knowledge to identify the most promising
areas to explore.

Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp

Day 5: Full-Day Game Drive in Northern Serengeti - Mara River Migration Experience

Today is dedicated to exploring the Northern Serengeti, with a special focus on the Mara River
and the Great Wildebeest Migration.

After an early breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive in search of the migrating herds. The
Northern Serengeti is one of the most dramatic locations to experience the migration, particularly
when large groups of wildebeest and zebras gather near the riverbanks.

The Mara River is a significant obstacle along the migration route. Its steep banks and flowing
water make crossing a challenging experience for the animals. When the herds decide to cross,
thousands of wildebeest may move towards the river, creating an unforgettable wildlife
spectacle. However, river crossings are natural events and cannot be guaranteed, as their timing
depends on rainfall, grazing conditions and the movement of the herds.

Beyond the migration, the Northern Serengeti is also home to resident predators. Lions may be
seen resting in the grasslands or watching the herds, while crocodiles inhabit sections of the
Mara River. Leopards and other wildlife may also be encountered around the riverine woodland.

Enjoy a picnic lunch in the wilderness and continue exploring different areas of the Northern
Serengeti throughout the day. Your guide will adjust the game-drive route according to wildlife
activity and the latest information about the migrating herds.

Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp
Day 6: Northern Serengeti - Central Serengeti - Ngorongoro Highlands

After breakfast, enjoy a morning game drive in the Northern Serengeti, providing another
opportunity to explore the migration area and search for wildlife before beginning your journey
south.

You will travel through the Central Serengeti, enjoying a scenic game drive across the vast plains
and wildlife-rich habitats. This route offers further opportunities to encounter the Serengeti's
resident animals, including elephants, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest and various predators.

The Central Serengeti is particularly recognised for its big-cat population. Its rocky kopjes, open
grasslands and surrounding woodlands provide important habitats for lions, leopards and
cheetahs. Keep your camera ready as your guide searches for interesting wildlife encounters
along the way.

Continue towards the Ngorongoro Highlands, where the scenery gradually changes from the
open Serengeti plains to cooler highland landscapes. The region is characterised by rolling hills,
lush vegetation, volcanic features and beautiful views of the surrounding conservation area.

Arrive at your accommodation in the Ngorongoro Highlands in the evening, where you can relax
and prepare for the following day's crater adventure.

Accommodation: Embalakai Ngorongoro Camp

Day 7: Ngorongoro Crater Game Drive

After an early breakfast, descend into the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable
game drive.

The Ngorongoro Crater is one of the world's most remarkable natural wildlife environments.
Formed by the collapse of a large volcanic mountain, the crater contains a variety of habitats,
including open grasslands, freshwater areas, woodland and marshes. These habitats support a
remarkable concentration of wildlife throughout the year.
During your game drive, explore the crater floor in search of the famous Big Five: lions,
elephants, buffaloes, leopards and the endangered black rhino. The crater is also home to zebras,
wildebeest, hippos, hyenas, jackals and numerous bird species.

One of the most special experiences is the opportunity to search for the black rhino, a rare and
protected species that makes the Ngorongoro Crater an important conservation destination.

Enjoy a picnic lunch at a designated site within the crater before continuing your wildlife
exploration. In the afternoon, ascend the crater walls and begin your return journey to Arusha or
transfer to the airport for your onward departure, depending on your travel arrangements.
