
7-Day February Calving Season Safari 
Experience the spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration during February’s calving season in the Southern Serengeti, with a special focus on the Ndutu Area. This 7-day safari is designed for travelers who want to spend more time in the heart of the calving grounds rather than moving between different regions of the Serengeti. 
Explore the Ndutu plains and surrounding Southern Serengeti, following large wildebeest herds and searching for newborn calves, while also looking for lions, cheetahs, hyenas and other predators attracted to the area. With several days dedicated to game drives around Ndutu, you have more time to observe the migration, wildlife behavior and unforgettable moments of the calving season. 
The safari also includes Tarangire for large elephants experience and the  Ngorongoro Crater, offering an opportunity to explore its remarkable wildlife and search for the Big Five, including the chance to see rhinos. This carefully designed journey combines February calving season, wildebeest migration, newborns, big cats and Ngorongoro Crater for an immersive Southern Tanzania wildlife experience. 
Day 1: Arrival in Arusha – Welcome & Safari Briefing 
Upon arrival in Arusha, you will meet our professional driver at the airport and transfer to your hotel. After check-in, you will have time to relax and prepare for your safari. 
You will also meet our safari consultant for a detailed safari briefing and meet your professional safari guide, who will introduce you to the safari program and provide important information about the journey ahead. Overnight in Arusha. 
Accommodation: Under the shade safari lodge 

Day 2: Arusha – Tarangire National Park – Wildlife Experience 
After breakfast, depart from Arusha and drive to Tarangire National Park for your first wildlife experience. Enjoy a game drive through the park, famous for its large elephant herds, impressive baobab trees and diverse wildlife. 
Your guide will search for elephants, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, antelopes, birds and, with luck, predators such as lions and cheetahs. Enjoy a picnic lunch during the day before continuing with your game drive. Later, proceed to your accommodation for dinner and overnight. 
Accommodation: Marera valley safari lodge 
Day 3: Tarangire – Southern Serengeti – Migration & Big Cats 
After breakfast, depart toward the Southern Serengeti and Ndutu Area. The journey takes you through changing landscapes as you approach the heart of the February calving season. 
Once you reach the Southern Serengeti, begin your game drive with a focus on following the Great Wildebeest Migration and searching for big cats. Look for large wildebeest herds, lions, cheetahs, hyenas and other wildlife that gather around the migration. Overnight in the Southern Serengeti/Ndutu Area. 
Accommodation: Safari haven Migration Camp 
Day 4: Full Day in Ndutu – Newborns & Great Migration 
Today is dedicated entirely to exploring the Ndutu Area during the spectacular calving season. Spend the full day following the migrating wildebeest herds and searching for newborn calves across the open plains. 
February is an exciting time to observe the interaction between the young animals and the predators that follow the herds. Your guide will search for lions, cheetahs, hyenas and other wildlife while giving you plenty of time for photography and wildlife observation. 
Enjoy a full-day game drive with a picnic lunch in the wilderness before returning to your accommodation for dinner and overnight. 
Accommodation: Safari haven Migration Camp 

Day 5: Full Day in Ndutu – Migration & Big Cats 
Enjoy another full day exploring the Ndutu Area, allowing you to experience the migration from different areas and follow the movement of the herds. 
Your main focus today will be the Great Wildebeest Migration and big cats. Search for lions, cheetahs and hyenas while following the wildebeest and observing their behavior during the calving season. Having a second full day in Ndutu gives you more time to explore different areas and increases your opportunities for memorable wildlife moments. 
Accommodation: Safari haven Migration Camp 
Day 6: Ndutu – Ngorongoro Crater – Arusha 
After an early breakfast, drive toward the Ngorongoro Crater and descend into the crater for an unforgettable wildlife experience. Explore the crater floor while searching for the Big Five, including lions, elephants, buffalo and, with luck, black rhinos. 
You may also encounter hippos, zebras, wildebeest, flamingos and many other species within this unique ecosystem. After the crater game drive, ascend from the crater and continue to Arusha for your final overnight. Accommodation: Under the Shade Safari Lodge 

Day 7: Departure – Airport Transfer 
Enjoy your final morning in Arusha according to your flight schedule. Depending on the time of your departure, you may have time to relax, explore Arusha or enjoy breakfast before your transfer. 
We provide a complimentary airport drop-off according to your flight schedule to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) or Arusha Airport (ARK).
