
12-Day Ultimate Tanzania Trip 
Discover the very best of Northern Tanzania on a 12-day private safari and cultural journey through some of the country’s most remarkable destinations. This adventure combines incredible wildlife, the Great Migration, spectacular landscapes and authentic cultural experiences. 
Begin your journey in Tarangire National Park, known for its giant elephant herds, ancient baobab trees and rich wildlife. Continue to the legendary Serengeti National Park, where you will spend several days exploring the endless plains and following the seasonal movement of the Great Wildebeest Migration. Enjoy unforgettable opportunities to see lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, buffalo, giraffes and other wildlife in their natural habitat. 
Your adventure continues to the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater, a natural wildlife haven where you can explore the crater floor and search for the Big Five, including the rare black rhino. The crater’s dramatic landscapes provide some of the most memorable views of your Tanzania journey. 
Beyond wildlife, discover the authentic side of Tanzania at Lake Eyasi. Meet local communities, learn about traditional lifestyles and experience a different side of Tanzania away from the main safari routes. The cultural experience adds a meaningful connection to the people, traditions and daily life of the region. 
With a private safari vehicle, professional guide and comfortable accommodation, this journey is designed to give you the freedom to explore at your own pace while enjoying personalized wildlife and cultural experiences. 
Day 1: Arrival in Arusha – Welcome to Tanzania 
Upon arrival in Arusha, you will be warmly welcomed by the Glad of Africa Safari & Tours team and transferred to your comfortable accommodation. After your journey, enjoy a relaxing evening and take time to settle into Tanzania. 
If your arrival time allows, you will meet your safari consultant and professional guide for a briefing about the adventure ahead. Your guide will introduce you to the safari route, daily activities and what to expect during your wildlife and cultural experiences. 
Overnight: Arusha 
Accommodation: Under the Shade Safari Lodge 

Day 2: Arusha National Park – Walking Safari & Mount Meru Experience 
After breakfast, depart for Arusha National Park, one of Tanzania’s most beautiful and diverse protected areas. Today offers a different way to experience Tanzania’s wilderness, with a guided walking safari accompanied by an armed park ranger. 
Walking through the park allows you to slow down and experience nature at a deeper level. Learn how to identify animal tracks, plants, birds and other signs of wildlife while enjoying the peaceful sounds of the forest. Depending on the area and conditions, you may encounter giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, warthogs and various antelope species. 
The park also offers spectacular views of Mount Meru, Tanzania’s second-highest mountain. Enjoy the beautiful scenery, forest environment and fresh mountain air before returning to Arusha. 
Overnight: Arusha 
Accommodation: Under the Shade Safari Lodge 
 
Day 3: Arusha to Tarangire National Park – Giants of the Baobab Country 
After breakfast, begin your safari journey toward Tarangire National Park, famous for its enormous elephant herds, ancient baobab trees and spectacular wildlife landscapes. 
Upon arrival, begin your game drive through the park. Tarangire’s seasonal rivers attract large numbers of wildlife, especially during the dry season. Look for elephants gathering around water, lions resting beneath trees, giraffes feeding among the acacias, and zebras, wildebeest, buffalo and antelopes moving across the plains. 
Your professional guide will take you through different areas of the park while explaining wildlife behavior, ecology and the importance of Tarangire to Tanzania’s ecosystem. The park is also excellent for birdwatching, with many colorful and unusual species to discover. 
After your game drive, continue to your comfortable lodge in the Karatu area for dinner and relaxation. 
Overnight: Karatu 
Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge 
 
Day 4: Karatu to Serengeti – Enter the Legendary Endless Plains 
After breakfast, depart Karatu and travel toward the world-famous Serengeti National Park. The journey takes you through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area before entering the vast Serengeti ecosystem. 
As you approach the Serengeti, the landscape transforms into endless golden plains. Begin your game drive as soon as you enter the park, with opportunities to see wildebeest, zebra, giraffe, elephants, gazelles and other wildlife. 
Your guide will also begin monitoring the seasonal location of the Great Wildebeest Migration. 
Depending on the time of year, you may already encounter large concentrations of migrating animals. 
Continue toward your accommodation while enjoying game viewing along the route. Arrive in the evening, enjoy dinner and relax under the African sky. 
Overnight: Serengeti National Park 
Accommodation: Safari Haven Camp 
 
Day 5: Full Day Serengeti – Following the Great Wildebeest Migration 
Today is dedicated to one of Tanzania’s greatest wildlife experiences — the Great Wildebeest Migration. 
After an early breakfast, set out with your professional guide for a full-day game drive. The goal is to follow the seasonal movement of the herds and spend time in areas where wildebeest and zebra are concentrated. 
The migration is a natural and constantly changing phenomenon. You may witness huge herds moving across the plains, crossing open grasslands or gathering around water sources. Wherever the herds are found, predators are often nearby, creating opportunities to see lions, cheetahs, hyenas and other carnivores. 
Enjoy a picnic lunch in the wilderness, allowing you to spend most of the day exploring without returning to camp. Your guide will use knowledge of the terrain, wildlife behavior and recent sightings to maximize your experience. 
Return to camp in the evening after a full day surrounded by the incredible landscapes of Serengeti. Overnight: Serengeti National Park 
Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp 
 
Day 6: Full Day Serengeti – Migration, Wildlife & Photography 
Continue your Serengeti adventure with another full day dedicated to following the migration. Having more than one full day in the ecosystem gives you additional time to explore different areas and observe the wildlife at a relaxed pace. 
Follow the movement of wildebeest and zebra while enjoying the incredible scenery of the Serengeti. Stop for photography whenever the opportunity arises and take time to appreciate the smaller details of the ecosystem, from birds and antelope to tracks and animal behavior. 
The Serengeti is much more than the migration. Your guide will also search for elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, hippos and other wildlife while keeping an eye out for predators following the herds. 
Every day in Serengeti is different, and the unpredictable nature of wildlife is what makes the experience so special. 
Overnight: Serengeti National Park 
Accommodation: Safari Haven Migration Camp 
 
Day 7: Full Day Serengeti – The Big Cat Experience 
Today, change the focus toward the famous big cats of the Serengeti. 
Set out early when predators are often most active. Explore areas where lions, cheetahs and leopards are commonly found, using your guide’s knowledge of animal behavior and recent sightings. 
Watch lions resting after a hunt, pride members interacting with one another, or cheetahs scanning the open plains for potential prey. With patience and luck, you may also encounter the elusive leopard resting in the branches of an acacia tree or moving quietly through the grass. 
Along the way, continue enjoying the Serengeti’s abundant wildlife, including elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeest and numerous bird species. 
This full day gives you time to enjoy the Serengeti beyond the migration and discover why it is one of Africa’s greatest destinations for predator viewing. 
Accommodation: Safari Haven migration Camp 
 
 
Day 8: Serengeti to Ngorongoro Highlands – From Endless Plains to the Highlands 
After breakfast, enjoy a final morning game drive in Serengeti as you begin your journey toward the Ngorongoro Highlands. 
The route provides another opportunity to enjoy wildlife before leaving the Serengeti. As you travel, the landscape gradually changes from open plains to greener highland scenery, with cooler temperatures and spectacular views. 
Arrive at your lodge in the afternoon and enjoy time to relax. The peaceful atmosphere of the Ngorongoro Highlands provides the perfect setting before tomorrow’s crater adventure. 
Overnight: Ngorongoro Highlands 
Accommodation: Embalakai Ngorongoro Camp 
 
Day 9: Ngorongoro Crater – Wildlife Experience & Lake Eyasi 
After an early breakfast, descend into the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater for a full wildlife experience. 
The crater is one of Tanzania’s most spectacular natural wonders and provides an incredible concentration of wildlife within its dramatic walls. 
Spend several hours exploring the crater floor in search of lions, elephants, buffaloes, hippos, zebras, wildebeest, hyenas and numerous bird species. Keep a special lookout for the endangered black rhino, one of the most memorable wildlife encounters in Tanzania. 
After your crater experience, ascend from the crater and continue toward Lake Eyasi, a remote and culturally fascinating part of northern Tanzania. 
Arrive at your accommodation and enjoy the peaceful surroundings of the lake region. Overnight: Lake Eyasi 
 Accommodation: Lake Eyasi Safari Lodge 
 
 
 
 
Day 10: Lake Eyasi – Hadzabe Cultural & Traditional Hunting Experience  
Wake early for a unique cultural experience with the Hadzabe community, one of Tanzania’s indigenous hunter-gatherer communities. 
Accompanied by local community members, learn about their traditional lifestyle and close relationship with the surrounding environment. Depending on the community activity that day, you may observe traditional hunting techniques, learn how natural resources are used, and gain insight into traditional knowledge passed from one generation to another. 
This is a very different experience from the classic safari and provides an opportunity to connect with Tanzania beyond its wildlife and landscapes. 
After the cultural experience, begin your journey toward Arusha. Enjoy the changing scenery along the way and arrive in the afternoon or evening for a relaxing overnight stay. 
Overnight: Arusha 
Accommodation: Under the shade Safari Lodge 
 
Day 11: Mount Kilimanjaro Day Hike – Shira One Experience 
Today is a special mountain adventure as you travel toward Mount Kilimanjaro for a day hike to Shira One. 
Start early and travel to the western side of Mount Kilimanjaro, where you begin your trek with a professional mountain guide. The route takes you into the spectacular high-altitude environment of the mountain, passing through changing vegetation and beautiful volcanic landscapes. 
Your hike takes you toward Shira One, one of the remarkable areas of the Kilimanjaro Shira Plateau. As you gain altitude, the scenery becomes increasingly dramatic, with wide-open landscapes and impressive views across the mountain environment. 
The Shira Plateau is one of Kilimanjaro’s most fascinating landscapes, offering a sense of adventure without requiring a full summit expedition. Take time to enjoy the scenery, capture photographs and appreciate the scale of Africa’s highest mountain. 
After spending time at Shira One, begin your descent and return toward the mountain gate before continuing to Moshi for a well-earned rest. 
Overnight: Moshi 
Accommodation: Chanya Lodge 
 
 
 
Day 12: Materuni Waterfall & Chagga Culture – Kilimanjaro Airport 
After breakfast, begin your final experience at Materuni Village, located on the slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro. 
Enjoy a guided walk through the beautiful village and coffee-growing areas while learning about the traditional Chagga culture. Discover how local coffee is grown, harvested and prepared, and experience the welcoming atmosphere of the community. 
Continue on a scenic walk toward Materuni Waterfall, one of the beautiful natural attractions around Kilimanjaro. Enjoy the peaceful surroundings, lush vegetation and spectacular views before returning to the village. 
If weather conditions are clear, take one final opportunity to admire the magnificent Mount Kilimanjaro before continuing to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your departure flight. 
Your 12-day Tanzania adventure ends with memories of wildlife, the Great Migration, Serengeti big cats, Ngorongoro, Lake Eyasi culture, Hadzabe traditions, Mount Kilimanjaro and Materuni Waterfall.
